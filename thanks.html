Bought Pro on another computer?

Your licence is tied to the install that paid for it. To use Pro here, restore it:

  1. Open your grade sheet.
  2. Gradewright → Already paid? → Restore a license.
  3. Paste the cs_ session ID from your Stripe receipt (or your licence key, if you saved it).

If you've lost both, email support@foxlayne.ai with your Stripe receipt and we'll move the licence.
