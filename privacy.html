Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. This page is written to be read in one minute. Gradewright is a Google Workspace add-on and website made by FoxLayne.

What leaves your document

Almost nothing. Gradewright runs inside your own Google account and asks for access to the current spreadsheet only. The only things ever sent to our server are:

  • a random install ID, created when you first open the add-on,
  • a one-way hash of your spreadsheet's ID, used only to count your one free class — the raw ID never leaves,
  • a coarse event or error code together with the add-on version,
  • a short ref value (the slug naming the page that sent the event), attached to checkout-start and page-view events, and
  • a Stripe checkout session ID, and only while you are buying Pro.

None of it is student data.

What never leaves your document

Student names, scores, grades, the number of students, your class name, email addresses and your IP address are never read off the sheet and never sent anywhere. Your spreadsheet's ID itself and its name are never sent — only a one-way hash of the ID, used to count your free class. We do not log IP addresses or user-agent strings. Your Google account identity is not collected.

The page-view counter

This website counts page views with a random ID stored in your browser's local storage, rotated every 24 hours. That lets us say "sessions" honestly, rather than "visitors". There are no cookies, no third-party scripts, no fonts or images loaded from other servers, and no advertising. Because there are no cookies and no cross-site identifiers, there is no cookie banner.

Retention

Events (a page view, a successful compute, an export) are kept for 90 days and then deleted. Only weekly counts survive past that, and those counts contain no names, no scores and no identifiers.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Gradewright never sees or stores your card details. Stripe's handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.
